About our Project

This project aims to provide additional information and numerical data regarding the sea shanties contained within The Music of the Waters by Laura Alexandrine Smith.

Our Research Question

Based on the Music of the Waters, what subjects appear and to what frequency in sailing songs?

For this project we used a digitized version of Music of the Waters from the Internet Archive which can be found HERE
