Song subject attributes — The schema specifies a controlled vocabulary for the subject attribute on <songTitle>. I mapped each song to the closest matching theme code:

wk (work) — keelmen's labour songs (Keel Row, Bonny Keel Laddie, Coaly Tyne, etc.)
hs (hardship) — press-gang songs (Captain Bover, Here's the Tender Coming, etc.)
dp (daily/place) — fishwives, fisher lads, street cries, angling songs
sl (sea/love) — songs about waiting for sailors, crossing the water
bt (boat) — the Row the Boat, Norman roundel
originTime — Most of these songs have no recorded composition date, so I used "N/A" as permitted by the schema. The one exception is the Row the Boat, Norman roundel, which is historically associated with 1453.

originLocation — I've tried to be as specific as possible (e.g. "Cullercoats, Northumberland" vs. "Tyneside" broadly) based on context clues in the prose.

Lyrics with music only — Several entries (Sandhill Corner, The Keelman Ower Land) are noted in the original as having music but no recorded lyrics. I've included a placeholder note rather than leaving the element empty, since the schema requires text content in <lyrics>.

Page breaks — I've used self-closing <pg id="..."/> elements at the points the original text marks new pages (108–125).
